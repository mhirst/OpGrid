# OpGrid roadmap

## Next

- **Hardware feedback.** First runs on a real Move: fix whatever turns up, measure CPU on the device.

## Planned

- **Web UI (`web_ui.html`).** A large editor in a phone, tablet or laptop browser, served by Schwung over Wi-Fi while the Move keeps playing:
  - every parameter with its full name and value, touch-controllable
  - the whole sequence and step locks at a glance
  - clip grid and song arranger
  - sound browser with search, saving and naming sounds and projects

## Later

- Clip follow actions (after N loops: next, previous, random, stop)
- Per-clip launch quantize
- MIDI clock out and per-track CC out
- Sample-playback machine
